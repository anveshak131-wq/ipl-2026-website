## Playing 11 Composition Rules

### Basic Team Composition
- **Playing XI**: Each team must field exactly 11 players during the match
- **T20 Format**: Each team plays 20 overs per innings
- **Support Staff**: Each team must have a coach and physiotherapist

### Overseas Player Regulations
- **Maximum in Playing XI**: No more than 4 overseas (foreign) players can be in the starting 11
- **Maximum in Squad**: No more than 10 overseas players in the total squad
- **Local Player Requirement**: At least 8 players should be from the local region of the team

### Age and Development Requirements
- **Under-22 Players**: At least 2 players should be from BCCI's under-22 pool
- **Capped vs Uncapped Players**: 
  - **Capped Player**: Has played for India in international cricket
  - **Uncapped Player**: Has not represented India internationally
  - **Capped Status Change**: A capped Indian player becomes uncapped if they haven't played in the starting XI in international cricket or don't have a BCCI central contract in the last 5 calendar years

## Impact Player Rule

### What is the Impact Player Rule?
The Impact Player rule allows teams to substitute one player from a pre-named list of five substitutes during a match. This tactical substitution can be used to adapt to match conditions and strengthen either batting or bowling mid-game.

### Key Features
- **Introduction**: First implemented in IPL 2023 season
- **Current Status**: Confirmed to continue through 2025-2027 cycle
- **Usage**: Optional - teams can choose whether to use it
- **Frequency**: Only one Impact Player can be used per team per match

### How It Works Step-by-Step

#### 1. Pre-Match Preparation
- **Before Toss**: Each team announces:
  - Playing XI (11 players)
  - List of 5 substitute players eligible to become Impact Player

#### 2. Substitution Timing
The Impact Player can be introduced at these specific times:
- **Before the start of an innings**
- **After the fall of a wicket**
- **At the end of an over**

#### 3. Implementation Process
- **Announcement**: Team must inform the fourth umpire before the player takes the field
- **Substitution**: The Impact Player replaces one of the original playing XI members
- **No Re-Entry**: Once replaced, the original player cannot return to the match

### Important Rules and Restrictions

#### Overseas Player Restrictions
- **4 Overseas Rule**: If a team already has 4 overseas players in the playing XI, the Impact Player cannot be an overseas player
- **Flexibility**: If fewer than 4 overseas players are in the XI, an overseas player from the substitutes can be used as Impact Player

#### Playing Capabilities
- **Full Participation**: Impact Player can bat, bowl, and field like any regular player
- **Batting Position**: Can bat at any position when introduced
- **Bowling Quota**: Can bowl full 4 overs even if the replaced player had already bowled

#### Captaincy Restrictions
- **No Captaincy**: Impact Player cannot act as captain during the match
- **Leadership**: Original captain must remain on field or be replaced by regular playing XI member

## Team Squad Requirements

### Squad Size and Composition
- **Total Squad Size**: Minimum 16 players per team
- **Maximum Squad Size**: Typically 25 players (including retentions and auction purchases)
- **Playing XI**: 11 players for each match
- **Substitute List**: 5 players named as potential Impact Players

### Player Categories
1. **Indian Capped Players**: Have represented India internationally
2. **Indian Uncapped Players**: Haven't represented India internationally
3. **Overseas Players**: Foreign nationals from cricket-playing nations
4. **Under-22 Players**: Young players in BCCI's development pool

## Player Acquisition Rules

### Acquisition Methods
1. **Annual Auction**: Primary method for acquiring new players
2. **Domestic Player Buying**: Direct acquisition of Indian domestic players
3. **Uncapped Player Signing**: Signing of uncapped Indian players
4. **Replacement Buying**: Acquiring replacements for unavailable players
5. **Trading**: Player exchanges between franchises

### Retention Rules (2025-2027 Cycle)
- **Maximum Retentions**: Teams can retain up to 6 players total
- **Retention Methods**: Combination of direct retentions and Right to Match (RTM) options
- **Capped Player Limit**: Maximum 5 capped players (Indian + Overseas) can be retained
- **Uncapped Player Limit**: Maximum 2 uncapped players can be retained

### Financial Regulations
- **Auction Purse 2025**: INR 120 Crore per franchise
- **Total Salary Cap**: 
  - 2025: INR 146 Crore
  - 2026: INR 151 Crore
  - 2027: INR 157 Crore
- **Components**: Auction purse + incremental performance pay + match fees

## Match Fee Structure

### New Match Fee System (2025-2027)
- **Match Fee Amount**: INR 7.5 Lakhs per match for each playing member
- **Coverage**: Includes Impact Players when used
- **Additional Payment**: Match fee is in addition to contracted salary
- **First Time**: This is the first time match fees have been introduced in IPL history

## Strategic Considerations

### Common Tactical Uses

#### Batting Impact Player
- **Purpose**: Strengthen batting lineup in chase situations
- **Timing**: Usually introduced during batting innings
- **Selection**: Often a power-hitter or finisher

#### Bowling Impact Player
- **Purpose**: Add bowling variety or strengthen death bowling
- **Timing**: Can be introduced during bowling innings
- **Selection**: Often a specialist bowler for specific conditions

#### All-Rounder Impact Player
- **Purpose**: Provide both batting and bowling options
- **Flexibility**: Can be used in either innings based on match situation

### Team Strategy Factors
1. **Match Conditions**: Pitch conditions, weather, dew factor
2. **Opposition Analysis**: Strengths and weaknesses of opposing team
3. **Toss Result**: Whether batting or bowling first
4. **Player Form**: Current form of key players
5. **Workload Management**: Managing player workload across tournament

### Examples from Real IPL Matches
- **Rohit Sharma (Mumbai Indians)**: Used as Impact Player in IPL 2025 for workload management
- **Death Bowling Specialists**: Often used as Impact Players in high-pressure situations
- **Power Hitters**: Frequently introduced during chase scenarios

## Comparison with Other Leagues

### Big Bash League (BBL) - X-Factor Rule
- **Similarity**: Allows tactical substitution
- **Key Differences**: 
  - BBL substitution only at 10-over mark
  - Replaced player must not have batted or bowled more than one over
  - More restrictive timing compared to IPL

### International Cricket
- **Concussion Substitutes**: Only for injury-related replacements
- **Regular Substitutes**: Can only field, cannot bat or bowl
- **No Tactical Substitutions**: International cricket doesn't allow tactical substitutions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is the Impact Player rule in IPL?
**A**: The Impact Player rule allows a team to substitute one player during a match from a list of five named substitutes. The substituted player can fully participate in batting, bowling, and fielding.

### Q2: When can the Impact Player be used?
**A**: An Impact Player can be introduced at the start of an innings, after a wicket falls, or at the end of an over.

### Q3: How many times can an Impact Player be used in one match?
**A**: Only one Impact Player can be used per team per match. The substitution is permanent for that match.

### Q4: Can an overseas player be an Impact Player?
**A**: Yes, but only if the team has used fewer than four overseas players in the starting XI.

### Q5: Is the Impact Player rule mandatory?
**A**: No, it's optional. Teams can choose whether to use the Impact Player based on match conditions and strategy.

### Q6: Can an Impact Player bat and bowl?
**A**: Yes, they can fully participate like any regular player, including batting and bowling their full quota.

### Q7: Why did MS Dhoni criticize the Impact Player rule?
**A**: MS Dhoni stated that the rule was unnecessary and reduces the importance of all-rounders in team composition.

### Q8: Can the replaced player return later?
**A**: No, once replaced, the original player cannot return to the match in any capacity.

### Q9: Does the Impact Player always get used?
**A**: Not always, but in over 90% of IPL matches, teams opt to use the substitution to gain a tactical advantage.

### Q10: Is this rule used in international cricket?
**A**: No, the Impact Player rule is exclusive to the IPL and some domestic Indian competitions as of now.
