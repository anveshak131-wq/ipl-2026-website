## 🎯 Quick Reference Guide

### Match Format

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Format** | T20 (20 overs per innings) |
| **Max Balls** | 120 balls per innings |
| **Max Wickets** | 10 wickets per innings |
| **Match Duration** | ~3.5 hours |
| **Teams (2025)** | 10 teams |
| **Teams (2026)** | 10 teams |
| **Matches (2025)** | 74 matches |
| **Matches (2026)** | 84 matches |

### Powerplay Rules

| Period | Overs | Fielders Outside 30-yard Circle |
|--------|-------|--------------------------------|
| **Powerplay** | 1-6 | Maximum 2 |
| **Middle Overs** | 7-15 | Maximum 4 |
| **Death Overs** | 16-20 | Maximum 5 |

### Strategic Timeouts

- **Number**: 2 per innings
- **Duration**: 2.5 minutes each
- **Purpose**: Tactical discussions and game planning

### DRS (Decision Review System)

- **Reviews per Innings**: 2 reviews per team
- **Reviewable Decisions**:
  - ✅ LBW
  - ✅ Caught
  - ✅ Run out
  - ✅ Stumped
  - ✅ Height wides (NEW in 2025)
  - ✅ Off-side wides (NEW in 2025)
  - ❌ Leg-side wides (NOT reviewable)

### Impact Player Rule

- **Substitutions**: 1 per match
- **When**: Can be made at any point
- **Must Be**: Named in squad before match
- **Purpose**: Strategic flexibility (batting/bowling)

### Two-Ball Rule (2025+)

- **When**: Evening matches only
- **Innings**: Second innings
- **Over**: From 11th over onwards
- **Condition**: Subject to umpires' assessment of dew
- **Purpose**: Counteract dew effects

### Over-Rate Penalties

| Offense Level | Penalty |
|---------------|---------|
| **Level 1** | 25-75% match fee fine + demerit points |
| **Level 2** | Higher fine + additional demerit points |
| **4 Demerit Points** | Can lead to match ban |
| **Validity** | 36 months (3 years) |

### Auction Rules

| Aspect | 2025 | 2026 |
|--------|------|------|
| **Salary Cap** | ₹120 crore | ₹120 crore |
| **Max Retention** | 6 players | No limit (within cap) |
| **RTM Option** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes |
| **Auction Type** | Mega Auction | Mini Auction |

## 🏏 Scoring Rules Quick Reference

### Regular Runs
- **0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6**: Standard runs from bat
- Counts toward: Team score, Batter runs, Bowler runs conceded

### Extras

| Type | Team Score | Batter Runs | Bowler Runs | Ball Counts? |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Wide (WD)** | +1 | +0 | +1 | ❌ No |
| **No Ball (NB)** | +1 | +0 | +1 | ❌ No |
| **Bye (B)** | +1-4 | +0 | +0 | ✅ Yes |
| **Leg Bye (LB)** | +1-4 | +0 | +0 | ✅ Yes |

### Combined Extras

| Type | Team Score | Batter Runs | Bowler Runs | Ball Counts? |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| **NB+1** | +2 | +1 | +2 | ❌ No |
| **NB+2** | +3 | +2 | +3 | ❌ No |
| **NB+4** | +5 | +4 | +5 | ❌ No |
| **NB+6** | +7 | +6 | +7 | ❌ No |
| **WD+1** | +2 | +1 | +2 | ❌ No |
| **WD+2** | +3 | +2 | +3 | ❌ No |
| **WD+4** | +5 | +4 | +5 | ❌ No |
| **2B, 3B, 4B** | +2, +3, +4 | +0 | +0 | ✅ Yes |
| **2LB, 3LB, 4LB** | +2, +3, +4 | +0 | +0 | ✅ Yes |

### Dismissal Types

1. **Bowled** - Ball hits stumps
2. **Caught** - Fielder catches on full
3. **LBW** - Leg Before Wicket
4. **Stumped** - Wicketkeeper removes bails
5. **Run Out** - Batter out of crease
6. **Hit Wicket** - Batter dislodges bails
7. **Obstructing Field** - Deliberate obstruction
8. **Handled Ball** - Illegal hand use
9. **Hit Ball Twice** - Strikes ball twice
10. **Timed Out** - Takes too long to reach crease

## 📊 Points System

| Result | Points |
|--------|--------|
| **Win** | 2 points |
| **Loss** | 0 points |
| **Tie** | 1 point each |
| **No Result** | 1 point each |

### Net Run Rate (NRR)

**Formula**: 
```
NRR = (Total Runs Scored / Total Overs Faced) - (Total Runs Conceded / Total Overs Bowled)
```

**Purpose**: Used to separate teams with equal points

## 🎯 Free Hit Rules

- **Triggered By**: No ball
- **Next Delivery**: Batter can only be dismissed by:
  - ✅ Run out
  - ✅ Hit wicket
  - ✅ Obstructing field
  - ✅ Handled ball
  - ✅ Hit ball twice
  - ❌ NOT by: Bowled, Caught, LBW, Stumped

## ⚠️ Important Regulations

### Bat Dimensions

- **Edge Thickness**: Maximum 4 cm
- **Overall Depth**: Maximum 6.7 cm
- **Enforcement**: In-game bat inspection system

### Ball Regulations

- **Standard**: Official IPL cricket ball (ICC specifications)
- **Two-Ball Rule**: See [Two-Ball Rule](#two-ball-rule-2025) above
- **Change**: Umpires can change if unfit for play

### Player Substitution

- **Impact Player**: 1 substitution per match
- **Mid-Season**: Replacement players allowed for injuries
- **Salary Cap**: Replacement fees excluded for that season

## 🔄 Rule Changes Timeline

### 2025 Changes

1. ✅ Saliva ban lifted
2. ✅ Two-ball rule introduced
3. ✅ DRS expanded (wides)
4. ✅ RTM option reintroduced
5. ✅ Retention limit: 4 → 6 players
6. ✅ Salary cap: ₹100 → ₹120 crore
7. ✅ Over-rate: No immediate bans
8. ✅ Bat inspection system
9. ✅ Demerit points: 36 months carry forward
10. ✅ Golden badge for champions

### 2026 Changes

1. ✅ Match expansion: 74 → 84 matches
2. ✅ Mini-auction (not mega)
3. ✅ No retention limit (within cap)
4. ✅ Stricter registration requirements
5. ✅ Two-year ban for unjustified withdrawals

## 🔍 Quick Lookup

### Common Questions

**Q: How many overs in IPL?**  
A: 20 overs per innings (120 balls maximum)

**Q: Can teams use DRS for wides?**  
A: Yes, for height and off-side wides only (from 2025)

**Q: What is the Impact Player rule?**  
A: Teams can substitute 1 player during a match

**Q: When is the two-ball rule used?**  
A: Evening matches, second innings, from 11th over (subject to dew)

**Q: What is the salary cap?**  
A: ₹120 crore per franchise (2025 & 2026)

**Q: How many players can be retained?**  
A: 6 players in 2025, no limit in 2026 (within squad size and cap)

**Q: What happens in a tie?**  
A: Super Over (unlimited until winner determined)

**Q: How long do demerit points last?**  
A: 36 months (3 years)
