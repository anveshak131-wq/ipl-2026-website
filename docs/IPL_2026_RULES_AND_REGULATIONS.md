## 🏆 Tournament Structure

### Match Format

- **Number of Teams**: **10 teams** competing
- **Total Matches**: **84 matches** (increased from 74 in previous season)
- **Tournament Duration**: 
  - **Start Date**: March 26, 2026
  - **End Date**: May 31, 2026
  - **Duration**: Approximately 2 months
- **Format**: Double round-robin + playoffs
- **Overs per Innings**: 20 overs (T20 format)
- **Maximum Balls**: 120 balls per innings

### Match Expansion Plan

- **2026**: 84 matches (current plan)
- **Future**: Plans to expand to **94 matches by 2028**
- **Goal**: Reinstating the complete double round-robin format
- **Rationale**: 
  - More matches = more revenue
  - Better competition
  - More opportunities for all teams

## 🎯 Auction and Player Registration Rules

### Mini-Auction Details

- **Date**: December 13-15, 2025
- **Venue**: Expected to be in India (facilitates smoother operations)
- **Type**: Mini-auction (not a mega auction)
- **Purpose**: Allows teams to fill gaps and make strategic additions

### Player Registration Requirements

#### Mandatory Registration

- **Foreign Players**: Overseas players **must register** for the mega auction to be eligible for the mini-auction
- **Purpose**: Ensures a streamlined selection process
- **Deadline**: Registration must be completed before the auction date

#### Eligibility Criteria

- **Previous Registration**: Players must have registered for the **2025 mega auction** to be eligible for 2026 mini-auction
- **Exceptions**: 
  - Legitimate injuries
  - Other approved reasons (subject to BCCI approval)

#### Penalties for Withdrawal

- **Two-Year Ban**: Players who withdraw from the 2025 season without legitimate reason or injury face a **two-year ban from IPL**
- **Impact**: 
  - Cannot participate in IPL 2026
  - Cannot participate in IPL 2027
  - Must wait until IPL 2028 to be eligible again
- **Purpose**: Ensures commitment and prevents last-minute withdrawals

### Salary Cap Components

- **Auction Purse**: Base amount available for player purchases
- **Incremental Performance Pay**: Additional payments based on performance
- **Match Fees**: Per-match payments to players
- **Total Cap**: ₹120 crore (carried forward from 2025)

## 🔄 Retention Rules

### Retention Deadline

- **Submission Date**: Franchises must submit their list of retained and released players by **November 15, 2025**
- **Purpose**: Allows time for auction preparation
- **Process**: 
  - Teams submit retention lists
  - Released players enter the auction pool
  - Mini-auction scheduled for mid-December

### Retention Flexibility

- **No Restriction**: Unlike mega auctions, there is **no restriction on the number of players** a team can retain before the mini-auction
- **Limitations**: 
  - Must adhere to **squad size limit of 25 players**
  - Must stay within **salary cap of ₹120 crore**
- **Strategic Advantage**: 
  - Teams can retain their core players
  - Focus on filling specific gaps in mini-auction
  - More strategic team building

### Squad Composition

- **Maximum Squad Size**: 25 players
- **Minimum Squad Size**: Typically 18-20 players
- **Overseas Players**: Maximum 8 overseas players in squad
- **Playing XI**: Maximum 4 overseas players in playing XI

## ⚽ In-Game Regulations

### Rules Carried Forward from 2025

Most in-game regulations from IPL 2025 continue into 2026:

#### Saliva Usage
- **Status**: Allowed (ban lifted in 2025)
- **Purpose**: Bowlers can maintain ball condition

#### Two-Ball Rule
- **Status**: Continues for evening matches
- **When**: Second innings, from 11th over onwards
- **Condition**: Subject to umpires' assessment of dew

#### DRS Expansion
- **Status**: Continues
- **Coverage**: Height wides, off-side wides
- **Limitation**: Leg-side wides not reviewable

#### Impact Player Rule
- **Status**: Continues
- **Usage**: One substitution per match allowed

#### Powerplay
- **Duration**: First 6 overs
- **Fielding Restrictions**: 2 fielders outside 30-yard circle

#### Strategic Timeouts
- **Number**: 2 per innings
- **Duration**: 2.5 minutes each

### Super Over Rules

- **Format**: One over per team (6 balls)
- **Unlimited**: No limit on number of Super Overs
- **Process**: Continues until a winner is determined

## ⚖️ Disciplinary Measures

### Suspension Points

- **Carry Forward Period**: **36 months (3 years)**
- **Previous System**: Points were reset each season
- **Current System**: Points accumulate and carry forward
- **Purpose**: 
  - Ensures sustained discipline
  - Maintains accountability across seasons
  - Prevents players from "resetting" each season

### Over-Rate Penalties

- **System**: Financial penalties + demerit points
- **No Immediate Bans**: Captains don't face immediate match bans
- **Accumulation**: 4 demerit points can lead to match bans
- **Validity**: Points valid for 36 months

### Code of Conduct

- **Standards**: Aligned with ICC Code of Conduct
- **Penalties**: 
  - Fines
  - Match bans
  - Suspension points
- **Appeals**: Players can appeal decisions through proper channels

## 🔄 Key Differences from 2025

### What's New in 2026

1. **Match Expansion**: 84 matches (up from 74)
2. **Mini-Auction**: Focus on strategic additions rather than complete rebuild
3. **Retention Flexibility**: No limit on retentions (within squad size and salary cap)
4. **Registration Requirements**: Stricter rules for foreign players
5. **Withdrawal Penalties**: Two-year ban for unjustified withdrawals

### What Continues from 2025

1. All in-game regulations (saliva, two-ball, DRS, Impact Player)
2. Salary cap of ₹120 crore
3. Suspension points carry forward for 36 months
4. Golden badge for defending champions
5. Over-rate penalty system
