## 🎯 Player Retention and Auction Rules

### Retention Policy

- **Maximum Retention**: Franchises can retain up to **6 players** before the auction
  - **5 capped players** (players with international experience)
  - **1 uncapped player** (players without international experience)
- **Uncapped Player Definition**: International players without a recent BCCI contract qualify as uncapped
- **Retention Deadline**: Teams must submit retention lists by the deadline specified by BCCI

### Right to Match (RTM) Option

- **Reintroduced**: The RTM card has been **reintroduced** for IPL 2025 (last used in 2017)
- **How It Works**:
  - Franchises can match the highest bid for a player who was part of their squad in the previous season
  - **New Rule**: Before a team can exercise its RTM card, the highest bidder is given an opportunity to **revise their bid**
  - This allows for competitive bidding while giving original teams a chance to retain players
- **Limitations**: RTM can only be used for players who were in the team's previous season squad

### Auction Purse

- **Salary Cap Increase**: The franchise purse has been raised from **₹100 crore to ₹120 crore** (20% increase)
- **Purpose**: Provides teams with greater financial flexibility during auctions
- **Components**: The salary cap includes:
  - Auction purse
  - Incremental performance pay
  - Match fees

### Uncapped Player Rule

- **Reinstated**: The uncapped player rule has been reinstated
- **Definition**: Permits franchises to retain international players without a BCCI contract under this category
- **Strategic Advantage**: Allows teams to retain promising young talent at lower costs

## ⚽ In-Game Regulations

### Saliva Ban Lifted

- **Status**: The prohibition on using saliva to shine the ball has been **lifted**
- **History**: This ban was implemented during the COVID-19 pandemic
- **Current Rule**: Bowlers are now permitted to use saliva for ball maintenance
- **Impact**: Allows bowlers to maintain ball condition, potentially aiding swing and reverse swing
- **Note**: This aligns with ICC's updated regulations

### Two-Ball Rule (Dew Management)

- **Purpose**: To counteract the effects of dew during evening matches
- **When Applied**: 
  - **Evening matches only** (does not apply to afternoon matches)
  - **Second innings** of evening matches
  - Starting from the **11th over** of the second innings
- **Process**:
  - The bowling team can request a ball change once after the 10th over
  - Umpires assess dew conditions
  - If conditions warrant, umpires replace the ball with one of similar wear and tear
  - A fresh ball is introduced from each end
- **Rationale**: Dew makes the ball slippery, disadvantaging bowlers; a fresh ball helps maintain grip and control

### Decision Review System (DRS) Expansion

- **New Capabilities**: Teams can now use DRS to challenge:
  - **Height wides**: Deliveries called wide due to height
  - **Off-side wides**: Deliveries called wide on the off-side
- **Limitations**: 
  - **Leg-side wides** remain at the discretion of on-field umpires (not reviewable)
- **Purpose**: Enhances accuracy of umpiring decisions and reduces controversial calls
- **Usage**: Each team has a limited number of reviews per innings (typically 2)

### Impact Player Rule

- **Status**: **Continues to be in effect** for IPL 2025
- **Introduced**: First introduced in IPL 2023
- **How It Works**:
  - Teams can substitute **one player** during a match
  - The substitution can be made at any point during the match
  - The Impact Player must be named in the squad before the match
  - Can be used strategically based on match situation (batting/bowling)
- **Strategic Use**: 
  - Teams often name an extra batter or bowler as Impact Player
  - Allows flexibility in team composition based on match conditions
- **Controversy**: Despite some criticism, the rule remains active

### Powerplay Rules

- **Duration**: First **6 overs** of each innings
- **Fielding Restrictions**: 
  - Maximum **2 fielders** allowed outside the 30-yard circle
  - Minimum **4 fielders** must be inside the 30-yard circle
- **Purpose**: Creates aggressive batting opportunities early in the innings

### Strategic Timeouts

- **Number**: Each team is permitted **2 strategic timeouts** per innings
- **Duration**: Each timeout is **2 minutes and 30 seconds** (2.5 minutes)
- **Purpose**: Allows teams to:
  - Discuss tactics and game plans
  - Regroup and refocus
  - Make strategic adjustments
- **Timing**: Teams can call timeouts at strategic moments during their innings

## ⏱️ Over-Rate and Discipline Rules

### Slow Over-Rate Penalties

- **No Match Bans**: Captains will **NOT face match bans** for slow over-rates
- **New System**: Instead, the following penalties apply:
  - **Financial penalties**: Fines based on the severity
  - **Demerit points**: Points assigned for slow over-rates
- **Demerit Points System**:
  - **Level 1 Offense**: Fine of 25% to 75% of match fees + demerit points
  - **Level 2 Offense**: Higher fine + additional demerit points (triggered after accumulating 4 demerit points)
  - **Validity**: Demerit points carry forward for **36 months (3 years)**
  - **Accumulation**: Accumulating 4 demerit points can lead to match bans
- **Rationale**: Provides more flexibility while maintaining discipline

### Suspension Points

- **Carry Forward**: Any suspension points incurred by a player now carry forward for **36 months**
- **Previous System**: Points were reset each season
- **Purpose**: Ensures sustained discipline and accountability across seasons
- **Impact**: Players must maintain discipline over multiple seasons

## 🏏 Equipment Regulations

### Bat Dimensions Enforcement

- **New System**: A new **in-game bat inspection system** has been introduced
- **Process**:
  - Umpires check each new batter's bat using a **specialized tool**
  - Inspection occurs when a new batter comes to the crease
  - Ensures compliance with size regulations
- **Limitations**:
  - **Edge thickness**: Maximum 4 cm
  - **Overall depth**: Maximum 6.7 cm
- **Purpose**: 
  - Addresses concerns over oversized "monster" bats
  - Ensures fair play and maintains balance between bat and ball
  - Prevents unfair advantage to batters

### Ball Regulations

- **Standard Ball**: Official IPL cricket ball as per ICC specifications
- **Two-Ball Rule**: See [Two-Ball Rule](#two-ball-rule-dew-management) section above
- **Ball Change**: Umpires can change the ball if it becomes unfit for play

## 🏆 Tournament Structure

### Match Format

- **Overs per Innings**: **20 overs** per team (T20 format)
- **Maximum Balls**: 120 balls per innings (20 overs × 6 balls)
- **Maximum Wickets**: 10 wickets per innings
- **Match Duration**: Approximately 3.5 hours per match

### Points System

- **Win**: 2 points
- **Loss**: 0 points
- **No Result**: 1 point each (if match abandoned)
- **Tie**: 1 point each (if scores are equal after 20 overs)

### Super Over Rules

- **When Used**: If scores are tied after both teams complete their 20 overs
- **Format**: One over per team (6 balls)
- **Process**:
  - Team that batted first in the match bats first in the Super Over
  - If still tied, additional Super Overs are played until a winner is determined
- **Note**: There is no limit on the number of Super Overs (unlike some other leagues)

### Playoff Structure

- **Qualifier 1**: 1st vs 2nd (winner goes to final)
- **Eliminator**: 3rd vs 4th (loser eliminated)
- **Qualifier 2**: Loser of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Eliminator (winner goes to final)
- **Final**: Winner of Qualifier 1 vs Winner of Qualifier 2

## 👥 Player Replacement Rules

### Mid-Season Replacements

- **Allowed**: Franchises can sign replacement players during the season
- **Salary Cap**: 
  - The league fee for replacement players is **excluded from the salary cap** for that season
  - If the contract is extended for subsequent seasons, the fee will be **included in the salary cap**
- **Conditions**: 
  - Replacement must be for an injured or unavailable player
  - Must be approved by IPL governing council
  - Replacement player must be registered and available

### Player Withdrawal

- **Penalties**: Players who withdraw from the season without legitimate reason or injury may face:
  - **Two-year ban** from IPL (for 2026 season eligibility)
  - Must have registered for mega auction to be eligible for mini-auction

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family and Travel Regulations

### Family Presence Rules

- **Stricter Regulations**: BCCI has implemented stricter rules regarding family members' access
- **PMOA Access**: Family members' access to Players and Match Officials Areas (PMOA) is now more restricted
- **Purpose**: Maintains professionalism and security during matches

### Travel Regulations

- **Mandatory Team Bus**: Teams must travel together on designated team buses
- **Designated Practice Nets**: Teams must use designated practice facilities
- **Purpose**: Ensures team unity, security, and fair access to facilities

## 🏅 Special Recognition

### Golden Badge for Defending Champions

- **Introduction**: Defending champions will wear a **'Golden Badge'** on their jerseys
- **Duration**: Throughout the following season
- **Purpose**: 
  - Marks distinction and achievement
  - Recognizes the previous season's champions
  - Adds prestige to the defending champions

## 📊 Alignment with ICC Rules

### Structural Changes

- **Alignment**: The IPL has undergone structural changes to align more closely with **International Cricket Council (ICC) regulations**
- **Purpose**: Ensures consistency with international cricket standards
- **Benefits**: 
  - Players experience similar rules in IPL and international cricket
  - Easier transition between formats
  - Maintains global cricket standards
