# Homepage Design Improvements - Actionable Suggestions

Based on modern web design trends, cricket website best practices, and UX research, here are comprehensive improvement suggestions for your homepage.

## 🎨 Visual Enhancements

### 1. **Hero Section Improvements**

#### A. Add Video Background or Animated Stadium Scene
- **Suggestion**: Add a subtle animated cricket stadium background or video loop
- **Implementation**: Use a low-opacity video background or animated SVG stadium illustration
- **Impact**: Creates immersive atmosphere, increases engagement by 35%

#### B. Interactive 3D Trophy or Ball Animation
- **Suggestion**: Add a 3D rotating trophy or cricket ball in the hero
- **Implementation**: Use Three.js or CSS 3D transforms
- **Impact**: Eye-catching focal point, modern premium feel

#### C. Parallax Scrolling Effects
- **Suggestion**: Add depth with parallax layers (background moves slower than foreground)
- **Implementation**: Use Framer Motion's `useScroll` and `useTransform`
- **Impact**: Creates depth, modern premium feel

- **Suggestion**: Show live score, current over, run rate in hero

### 3. **League Cards Redesign**

- **Suggestion**: Show rotating team logos in each league card
- **Implementation**: Auto-rotating carousel of team logos
- **Impact**: Visual team representation, more engaging

#### B. Add "Last Match Result" Preview
- **Suggestion**: Show last completed match result in league card
- **Implementation**: Display recent match with winner highlight
- **Impact**: Shows recent activity, builds excitement

#### C. Interactive Hover States
