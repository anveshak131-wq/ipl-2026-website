# Resend Email Verification Guide

## Two Options in Resend

When setting up Resend, you'll see two options:

### Option 1: "I own a domain" (Recommended) ✅
- Verify your entire domain
- Use any email from that domain (e.g., `noreply@yourdomain.com`, `hello@yourdomain.com`)
- Best for production
- Requires domain ownership

### Option 2: "I don't own a domain" (Email Verification)
- Verify a specific email address
- Can only use that exact email address
- Good for testing, but may still have limitations
- Doesn't require domain ownership

## If You Don't Have a Domain

Choose: **"I don't own a domain"**

### Steps:
1. Click **"I don't own a domain"**
2. Enter an email address you control (e.g., your Gmail)
3. Resend will send a verification email
4. Click the verification link in your email
5. Once verified, you can use that email as "from" address

### Limitations:
⚠️ **Important**: Even with email verification, Resend's free tier may still:
- Limit sending to your account owner email only, OR
- Allow sending to all users (depending on Resend's policy)

**You'll need to test** to see if it works for sending to all users.
